# Mi Moda Privacy Policy

Mi Moda is a fashion social app that lets users create profiles, post outfit photos, participate in quick opinion voting, and comment on threads.

## Information we collect
- account details such as email, username, and password (stored securely via the app's auth provider)
- profile information and username display name
- outfit captions, comments, and votes
- uploaded images and related metadata for social features

## How we use information
- create and manage user accounts
- power the social feed and discussion threads
- enable voting and engagement experiences
- improve moderation and platform safety

## Data sharing
We do not sell personal information. We may share selected data with trusted backend providers required to operate the app, including authentication and storage providers.

## User rights
Users can request access to, correction of, or deletion of their personal data through the support contact linked in the app metadata.

## Security
We use secure authentication and storage best practices, including encrypted session storage and backend-managed user data.

## Contact
For privacy or account requests, contact support@yourbrand.com.
